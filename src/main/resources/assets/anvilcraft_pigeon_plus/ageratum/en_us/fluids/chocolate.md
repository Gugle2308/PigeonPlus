# Liquid Chocolate

<item id="anvilcraft_pigeon_plus:liquid_chocolate_bucket"/>

Liquid chocolate is the molten form of chocolate. It comes in plain, dark, and white, matching the base mod's chocolate blocks.

## Melting

Place a heater below a large cauldron and heat chocolate blocks to melt them into the matching liquid chocolate. Each block yields **1000 mB**.

| Input | Output |
| --- | --- |
| Chocolate Block | Liquid Chocolate 1000 mB |
| Black Chocolate Block | Liquid Dark Chocolate 1000 mB |
| White Chocolate Block | Liquid White Chocolate 1000 mB |

## Blending

You can skip making chocolate blocks and blend the ingredients directly in a cauldron.

- Liquid Chocolate: Cocoa Liquor ×8 + Cocoa Butter ×4 + Cream ×4 + Sugar ×2
- Liquid Dark Chocolate: Cocoa Liquor ×12 + Cocoa Butter ×4 + Sugar ×2
- Liquid White Chocolate: Cocoa Butter ×12 + Cream ×4 + Sugar ×2

Each batch yields **1000 mB**.

::: warning Requires heat
These recipes all require heat: an air blender must be fitted with a blaze head, while a powered electric blender is enough on its own. See [Air Blender](../devices/blender) and [Electric Blender](../devices/electric_blender).
:::

## Solidifying

Liquid chocolate left to settle and cool in a cauldron solidifies back into the matching chocolate block: **1000 mB** yields **1** chocolate block.

## Filling Cookie Pillars

Right-click a cookie pillar with a bucket of liquid chocolate to fill it into a chocolate sandwich cookie pillar. See [Chocolate Sandwich Cookie Pillar](../devices/chocolate_cookie_pillar).
