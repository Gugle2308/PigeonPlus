# Chocolate Sandwich Cookie Pillar

<block id="anvilcraft_pigeon_plus:chocolate_cookie_pillar"/>

A cookie pillar is hollow inside. Fill that hollow with liquid chocolate and it becomes a chocolate sandwich cookie pillar, in plain, dark, and white variants.

## Filling

Right-click a cookie pillar with a bucket of liquid chocolate to fill it.

<block id="anvilcraft:cookie_pillar"/>

## Emptying

Right-click the filled pillar with an empty bucket to draw back one bucket of the matching liquid chocolate; the pillar reverts to a plain cookie pillar.

## Leaking

The chocolate inside leaks out of the pillar's **two axial ends**.

## Drops and Pick Block

The filled pillar has no item form and can only be obtained by filling a cookie pillar. Breaking it drops a **plain cookie pillar**, and pick block likewise gives only a cookie pillar.
