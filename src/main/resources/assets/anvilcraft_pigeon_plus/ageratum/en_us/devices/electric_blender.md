# Electric Blender

<block id="anvilcraft_pigeon_plus:electric_blender"/>

The electric blender is the powered version of the air blender. It needs no compressed air and runs on grid power instead.

## Differences from the Air Blender

- It runs as soon as it is powered, with no compressed air to pump in.
- While running it **supplies heat upward**, so it can act as both the blender and the heat source, with no separate heater needed.

## Heating

The electric blender supplies heat upward while powered, so for recipes that need both blending and heating — such as biogas conversion — a single electric blender satisfies every condition on its own. See [Biogas](../fluids/biogas).

For the same reason it **places no restriction on the blender head** for heat-requiring blending recipes such as [liquid chocolate](../fluids/chocolate); an air blender, by contrast, must be fitted with a blaze head.

## Crafting

The electric blender is made from a heater and a magnetoelectric core through multiblock crafting. See the "Multiblock" category in JEI for the exact steps.
