# Large Fireworks Rocket

<block id="anvilcraft_pigeon_plus:large_fireworks_rocket"/>

The large fireworks rocket is a 3×3×9 multiblock. Once lit it climbs into the sky trailing flame.

## Building

It is built from paper blocks, gunpowder blocks, stripped oak logs and other materials. See the "Multiblock" or "Multiblock Conversion" category in JEI for the exact shape and material list.

## Loading

Right-click the main part to load contents into it:

- **Firework Star**: stores its explosion in the rocket, up to **16**. They all go off when the rocket detonates. Notably, a single firework star can burst into 4 firework balls.
- **Ender Pearl**: up to **4**, each bound to the player who put it in.

<item id="minecraft:firework_star"/>
<item id="minecraft:ender_pearl"/>

## Launching

Right-click the rocket with flint and steel to light it. The rocket rises straight up from where the main part is.

You can also ride it and use it as a vehicle.

## Traveling to the Moon

While climbing, the rocket may fly to the moon if both conditions hold:

- It reaches an altitude of about **512 blocks**.
- The time is midnight.

When the conditions are met the chance is about **50%**. If ender pearls are loaded, the players those pearls belong to are each sent to the moon. On failure the rocket does not stop — it keeps climbing and detonates as usual when the fuse runs out.

On arrival the landing spot is roughly 8~16 blocks from the moon's spawn point.

## Dismantling

An anvil hammer can take the rocket apart. Dismantling returns the loaded firework stars and ender pearls.
